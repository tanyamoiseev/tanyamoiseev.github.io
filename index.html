About the artist

Tanya Moiseev

Tanya Moiseev creates art with an instinct for color, texture, and storytelling. Moving freely between oil, acrylic, watercolor, and mixed media, she draws inspiration from the natural world, the character of animals, the movement of the sea, and the expressive possibilities of the human form.

Her paintings often balance the familiar with the unexpected. A quiet animal becomes a study in personality, a coastal scene captures both movement and refuge, while flowers, faces, and abstract forms emerge through layers of color, pattern, and texture. Whether bold and energetic or calm and contemplative, each work reflects Tanya's curiosity and willingness to follow where the creative process leads.

For Tanya, painting is less about reproducing what is seen and more about expressing what is felt. Art From The Heart celebrates that spirit—a collection created with imagination, emotion, experimentation, and the simple joy of making something meaningful.

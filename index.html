About the artist

Tanya Moiseev

Tanya paints from a place of curiosity, feeling, and delight in the creative process. Her work is inspired by the beauty found in everyday moments—light across a landscape, the energy of flowers, the movement of color, and the emotions that can be expressed without words.

For Tanya, painting is both discovery and connection. Each canvas is an invitation to slow down, look closely, and experience color in a personal way. “Art From The Heart” reflects her belief that creativity is most meaningful when it is sincere, joyful, and shared.

— Tanya Moiseev

About the Artist

Tanya Moiseev is a passionate artist whose work reflects a deep appreciation for beauty, emotion, and imagination. Through her paintings, she seeks to capture moments, moods, and stories that resonate with viewers on a personal level.

Working primarily in oil on canvas, Tanya draws inspiration from nature, maritime themes, and the world around her. Her artistic style combines careful attention to detail with a creative interpretation of light, color, and atmosphere.

Each piece is created with the belief that art has the power to inspire, evoke memories, and bring warmth into everyday life. Through Art From The Heart, Tanya shares her creative journey and invites others to experience the emotions and stories behind each work.

About the Artist

Tanya Moiseev is an artist whose work explores the relationship between emotion, memory, and the natural world. Her paintings often feature maritime scenes, landscapes, and subjects that inspire reflection and connection.

Based in Florida, Tanya creates original artwork that combines technical craftsmanship with heartfelt expression. Drawing inspiration from her surroundings and life experiences, she strives to produce pieces that are both visually engaging and emotionally meaningful.

Through her online gallery, Art From The Heart, Tanya shares a collection of original works created to bring beauty, inspiration, and a personal touch to homes and collections.

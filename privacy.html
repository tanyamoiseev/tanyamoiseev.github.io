Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 9, 2026

Art From The Heart is the online gallery of Tanya Moiseev. We respect your privacy and use information you provide to respond to your inquiries about artwork and purchases.

Information you provide

When you use our contact form, we collect the name, email address, optional artwork title, and message you submit. Please do not submit payment card details or other sensitive information through this form.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to questions, discuss availability and purchase arrangements, and maintain relevant correspondence. We do not use the inquiry form to subscribe you to marketing emails.

Form processing and spam protection

Our contact form is processed by Web3Forms, which forwards submissions to Tanya's email. We also use hCaptcha to reduce automated submissions. These providers may process technical information as described in their own privacy notices. Our website does not collect payment information through the contact form.

Retention

Form submission retention in Web3Forms is controlled by its account settings. Correspondence delivered to Tanya's email may be retained as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries and maintain transaction-related records. You may contact Tanya to request deletion of correspondence, subject to applicable recordkeeping obligations.

Sharing

We do not sell contact form information. Information is shared with the service providers needed to process and deliver the inquiry, and otherwise only when required by law or necessary to fulfill your request.

Your choices

You may contact Tanya to ask about information you submitted or request its correction or deletion where applicable.

Updates

We may update this policy when our website or contact services change. The effective date above indicates the current version.
